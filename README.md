## Investigation Methodology

Depending on the project, investigations and detection labs document:

1. Incident or lab overview and investigation objectives
2. Tools, techniques, and supporting evidence
3. Artifact analysis, threat intelligence enrichment, or detection rule development and validation
4. Indicators of compromise (IOCs), where supported by evidence
5. MITRE ATT&CK mapping, where supported by evidence
6. Findings, alert counts, severity and verdict where applicable, and investigation limitations
7. Recommended containment, remediation, further investigation, or detection tuning, where applicable
8. Lessons learned
